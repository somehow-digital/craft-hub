title: Users
description: Users, their groups and permissions.
---

_Data → Users_ lists the users, with their user groups above.

## List

**Columns**: status, email, groups, the number of entries they're an author of, whether they've set up two-step verification (an authenticator app or a passkey), last login and creation date.

**Search** by name, username or email.

**Filter** by:

- status, group (or no group) and how recently they signed in
- two-step verification
- number of entries and creation date
- admins and unverified email changes

The **user groups** above the list show their users, permissions and whether they require two-step verification.

## Detail page

- The user's account details.
- Their permissions, and whether they're granted directly or through a group.
- The entries they're an author of and the files they uploaded.
- The elements referencing them and those they reference.
- What they [maintain](/features/maintainers/), themselves or through their groups.

:::note
Users aren't site-specific, so they aren't scoped to the selected sites.
:::

## Diagnostics

Locked users, admins without two-step verification, idle, pending and suspended users, and unverified email changes.
