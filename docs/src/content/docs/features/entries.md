_Data → Entries_ lists the entries of all sections.

## List

**Columns**: section, entry type, status, creator, references, post date and last edit. Titles link to the entry's edit page, and the last column to its section's preview targets (a menu when there are several).

**Search** by title or slug.

**Filter** by:

- origin (section or nested entry field), entry type and status
- author and who last edited them
- whether they're referenced, stale, or have drafts
- whether they have drafts or unsaved changes of the user viewing the list

## Detail page

- The entry on each site, and when and by whom it was last edited.
- The elements referencing it and those it references.
- A **History** tab with its saved drafts and, for entries with versioning, its revisions (the current one marked, with creators and notes).

The _View entry_ button follows the same preview targets as the list.

## Diagnostics

Unreferenced, stale and disabled entries, and entries with drafts.
